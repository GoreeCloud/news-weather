# GoreeCloud News & Weather — Branding

## Canonical identity

- **Canonical product name:** GoreeCloud News & Weather
- **Installed application name:** News & Weather
- **Repository:** GoreeCloud/news-weather
- **Candidate application identifier:** com.goreecloud.newsweather

## Naming use

Use **GoreeCloud News & Weather** where ownership context matters, including repository documentation, product pages, release metadata, and formal references.

Use **News & Weather** for concise installed-app and launcher presentation.

## Product tone

The product should feel:

- Calm.
- Direct.
- Useful.
- Privacy-focused.
- Uncluttered.
- Non-promotional.

## Visual system

Glaze V1.7 / 1.7.0 is the current Official Anchor and consumer-eligible GoreeCloud design-and-experience-system target.

News & Weather does not inherit Glaze acceptance automatically. This repository must complete fresh local Glaze 1.7.0 adoption and acceptance evidence before conformance can be claimed.

## Iconography

Final product iconography is not yet established. Do not use unrelated third-party news or weather branding.
