# GoreeCloud News & Weather — Benefits

GoreeCloud News & Weather is intended to provide a focused alternative to attention-driven news and weather products.

## User benefits

- **Selected sources instead of behavioral ranking** — news comes from sources the user chooses.
- **Chronological reading by default** — ordering remains understandable.
- **No advertising** — interface space is reserved for news, weather, and controls that directly support them.
- **No mandatory account** — basic use remains available locally.
- **Manual weather locations** — GPS permission is not a prerequisite for checking weather.
- **Privacy-first current location** — location access is explicit and purpose-limited.
- **Clear weather freshness** — cached forecasts must show when they were last updated.
- **Bounded Home** — the overview answers what is happening and what the weather is without becoming an infinite dashboard.
- **Cross-platform direction** — one shared application foundation targets Android, Web, Linux, and Windows.
- **GoreeCloud integration without database coupling** — platform capabilities are consumed through governed contracts.

These are product objectives. They become delivered benefits only as the corresponding implementation is verified.
