# GoreeCloud News & Weather — Competitive Objectives

This project does not compete by maximizing time-on-screen.

## Objectives

GoreeCloud News & Weather should outperform common ad-supported news/weather experiences in the areas that matter to this product:

- Privacy.
- Absence of advertising and sponsored placement.
- User control over news sources.
- Understandable chronological ordering.
- Low interface clutter.
- Conservative notifications.
- Manual-location usability.
- Clear offline and stale-data behavior.
- Accessibility.
- Cross-platform consistency.
- Self-hosted GoreeCloud integration.
- Data portability and replaceable provider architecture.

## Non-objectives

The project does not seek advantage through:

- Engagement-maximizing feeds.
- Behavioral profiling.
- Outrage or controversy ranking.
- Generic recommendation volume.
- Infinite-scroll retention.
- Sponsored discovery.
- Artificial urgency.
- Forced account creation.

## Evaluation rule

Competitive claims must be tied to implemented and verified behavior. Planned features alone do not establish superiority over another product.
